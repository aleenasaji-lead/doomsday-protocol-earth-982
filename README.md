# DoomCopy Threat Analysis

DoomCopy could imitate Apple's social media advertisements, website layouts, product photography style, and general campaign ideas. However, it would be harder to reproduce Apple's established reputation, proprietary technology, and integration between Apple devices and services.

**Marketing response:** Apple should communicate genuine product benefits, its ecosystem, privacy features, and customer experience. These are more difficult to imitate than the appearance of an advertisement.

Note: DoomCopy is treated as a hypothetical competitor for this analysis.

# North Star Metric and SMART KPIs

**North Star metric:** Number of completed online purchases attributed to digital marketing channels.

**KPI 1 — Conversion rate:** Increase the product-page purchase conversion rate by 10% relative to baseline within 100 days.

**KPI 2 — Organic search:** Increase qualified organic search visits to product pages by 15% within 100 days.

**KPI 3 — Product comparison:** Increase product comparison engagement by 10% relative to baseline within 100 days.

**Measurement:** Record baseline performance before the 100-day period and compare results at the end. These are proposed assignment targets, not Apple's actual internal targets.
